## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Recognition by Sovereign State

Swahili (*Kiswahili*) is not the language of a single country. It is the premier indigenous lingua franca of Sub-Saharan Africa and an official working language of the **African Union (AU)**, **East African Community (EAC)**, and **Southern African Development Community (SADC)**.

| Country | Legal Status | Primary Dialects / Variants | Key Economic & Institutional Context |

## 2. Dialectal & Linguistic Variation Taxonomy

### 2.1 Kiswahili Sanifu (Standard Swahili)
- **Geographic Center:** Tanzania, Kenya public administration, EAC headquarters (Arusha).
- **Phonology & Grammar:** Standard 18-noun class agreement system (*ngeli*), strict concordial agreement, rich inflectional morphology.
- **Institutions:** BAKITA (Baraza la Kiswahili la Taifa, Tanzania), CHAKITA (Chama cha Kiswahili cha Taifa, Kenya).

### 2.2 Kingwana (Congolese Swahili)
- **Geographic Center:** Eastern & Southern DRC (Goma, Bukavu, Lubumbashi, Kindu, Kisangani, Bunia).
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - **French Lexical Blends:** Extensive use of French loanwords for modern concepts (*bureau*, *carte*, *l'argent*, *commune*, *moteur*, *chauffeur*).
  - **Phonetic Shifts:** Frequent interchange of `/l/` and `/r/` (e.g., *kuludia* instead of *kurudi*; *kuvunja* / *kupasula*).
  - **Verb Prefixing:** Distinct tense markers in colloquial speech (e.g., prefixing with *bina-* or *tuko na...*).
  - **French Time-Telling:** Often tell time using the European 24-hour / 12-hour clock rather than traditional Swahili time (*saa moja asubuhi* vs *saa saba ya mchana*).

### 2.3 Sheng (Nairobi Urban Slang)
- **Geographic Center:** Nairobi, Nakuru, Mombasa (Kenya) and spreading to Kampala (Uganda).
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Fast-evolving sociolect combining Swahili syntax with English, Kikuyu, Dholuo, and Luhya vocabulary.
  - Core terms: *mbogi* (group/crew), *ganji* / *chapaa* (money), *luku* (clothing/drip), *rada* (alert/aware), *nduthi* (motorcycle/bodaboda), *base* (hangout spot).

### 2.4 Ugandan Swahili
- **Geographic Center:** Kampala, Jinja, Entebbe, Gulu, Mbale.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - High degree of English and Luganda integration.
  - Honorifics and market terminology (*ssebo*, *nyabo*, *katale* = soko, *bodaboda*).
  - Direct pragmatic phrasing with reduced noun-class concordial complexity.

### 2.5 Great Lakes Border Swahili (Rwanda & Burundi)
- **Geographic Center:** Rubavu/Goma border, Rusizi/Bukavu border, Bujumbura Buyenzi.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Blending of Swahili with Kinyarwanda/Kirundi terms (*amazi*, *umusaza*, *abaturage*).
  - Fast commercial exchange phrasing focused on cross-border customs declarations and currency exchange.

## 4. Architectural Requirements for Pan-African Serving

To serve all Swahili-speaking populations without discrimination or geographic bias, LughaLLM must adhere to 5 architectural mandates:

1. **Multi-Dialect Recognition:** Dialect detector must classify between `kiswahili_sanifu`, `sheng_nairobi`, `kingwana_drc`, `swahili_uganda`, and `coastal_swahili`.
2. **Context-Sensitive Currency & Tax Mapping:** When a user mentions tax, the system must not assume Tanzania (TRA); it must query or resolve the country (KRA for Kenya, DGI for DRC, URA for Uganda, RRA for Rwanda).
3. **Multi-Currency Token Pricing:** Token ledger must support pricing in TZS, KES, UGX, CDF, RWF, and BIF.
4. **Inclusive Agricultural Models:** Farming advisors must account for agro-ecological zones from the volcanic soils of North Kivu to the Ugandan highlands, Kenya's Rift Valley, and Southern Tanzania.
5. **Data Sovereignty Compliance Across Blocs:** Must honor Kenya DPA, Tanzania PDPC, Uganda Data Protection & Privacy Act 2019, and DRC Law No. 20/017 on telecommunications and information technologies.
